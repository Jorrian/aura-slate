Support

Last updated: July 25, 2026

Aura Slate is a field-first home inspection app for iPhone and iPad. These steps cover the most common setup, report, synchronization, and subscription questions.

Start or resume an inspection

  • Open the Home workspace and create or select an inspection.
  • Confirm the property, client, inspector, and scope information.
  • Follow the physical route through the property while adding findings.
  • Use the report workspace to review summary inclusion and PDF preflight.

Aura Slate preserves report order separately from field capture order, so you can move through the property efficiently without reorganizing the final report by hand.

Photos, dictation, and site features

Camera, photo-library, microphone, speech-recognition, location, and notification features require the corresponding Apple permission. Review or change permissions in the Settings app on your device. Core finding entry and report review remain available without optional dictation, location, or reminder access.

PDF publishing

  • Resolve material preflight items before relying on a client-ready report.
  • Check the executive summary, limitations, inspector identity, and lead photos.
  • Preview the PDF before choosing an export or share destination.
  • Verify the recipient before sending client or property information.

Solo subscription and restore

Built-in example reports remain available without a subscription. Aura Slate also permits three new live inspections to publish before Solo is required for additional new client PDFs.

  • Open Settings and choose the Solo plan surface.
  • Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account used for the subscription.
  • Use Manage Subscription to review or cancel through Apple.
  • If live localized prices do not load, purchase controls stay unavailable; try again when App Store services are reachable.

iCloud synchronization

  • Confirm that iCloud is enabled for Aura Slate on each device.
  • Use the same Apple Account and install the current app version.
  • Allow each device time to connect after opening the app.
  • Do not delete the only verified copy of active inspection work while troubleshooting synchronization.

Contact support

Email jorrian@mac.com and include:

  • device model and operating-system version;
  • Aura Slate version and build from Settings;
  • a short description and the steps that led to the issue; and
  • whether the issue involves capture, PDF export, iCloud, or StoreKit.

Do not email client reports, private inspection photos, payment information, passwords, or Apple Account credentials unless a secure, task-specific support path is explicitly arranged.

Aura Slate supports inspection documentation; it does not replace professional judgment, applicable standards of practice, or regulatory requirements.
