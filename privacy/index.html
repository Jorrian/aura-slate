Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Aura Slate is designed to keep inspection data in your control. This policy explains the information the app handles and the Apple services it uses to provide its features.

Information you create

Aura Slate stores information you enter or attach to an inspection, which may include:

  • property, client, agent, and inspector details;
  • findings, narratives, recommendations, limitations, and report notes;
  • inspection photos, markup, and imported floor plans;
  • report preferences, inspector profiles, and workflow position; and
  • optional site check-in and reminder information.

You decide what client and property information to enter. Do not add information that is unnecessary for the inspection or report.

How information is used

Aura Slate uses this information to:

  • save and resume inspection work;
  • organize findings and photos;
  • create deterministic summaries and PDF reports;
  • support optional arrival reminders and site check-ins; and
  • restore subscription entitlement through StoreKit.

Storage and synchronization

Inspection content is stored on your device. When iCloud is available and enabled for Aura Slate, compatible content may synchronize through your private iCloud database so it is available on your devices. Apple operates iCloud and controls its account, retention, and security services.

PDF reports and other files you export leave Aura Slate only when you choose a destination through an Apple sharing or file interface. You are responsible for choosing an appropriate recipient and storage location for client reports.

Apple services and device permissions

Aura Slate may use Apple frameworks for:

  • iCloud and CloudKit synchronization;
  • StoreKit subscription purchase, verification, and restore;
  • camera and photo-library access initiated by you;
  • microphone and speech recognition for optional dictation;
  • location, maps, and notifications for optional site features; and
  • on-device system intelligence features when supported and requested.

Permission-dependent features remain unavailable if you decline the corresponding permission. Apple processes information used by its services under Apple's applicable privacy terms.

Purchases

Apple processes App Store payments. Aura Slate receives transaction and entitlement status needed to provide and restore the Pro subscription, but it does not receive your full payment-card details.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Aura Slate does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not track you across apps or websites. The shipped privacy manifest declares no tracking or collected data types.

Website

This website does not collect email addresses, provide a mailing-list form, or use third-party analytics. Contact links open your email app; you choose whether to send an enquiry. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose handling of visitor connection information is described in GitHub's privacy statement. An older website preview may have stored an email address locally in your browser without submitting it; you can remove that record by clearing this site's browser storage.

Deletion and retention

Remove content you no longer want with the editing and removal controls available for that content, or remove the app's local data by uninstalling it. Content that has synchronized through iCloud may remain in your private iCloud storage until it is deleted through an available app control or Apple's iCloud data controls. Exported reports remain wherever you chose to save or share them.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email jorrian@mac.com.

Aura Slate is intended for professional inspection documentation. An inspector remains responsible for protecting client information and selecting appropriate recipients for exported reports.
